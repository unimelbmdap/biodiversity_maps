Melbourne’s native bee highways

This map predicts where five native bee species are likely to turn up across the City of Melbourne. It draws on a decade of fieldwork that logged more than 5000 visits by bees to flowers.

How to explore

  • Tap a bee’s name along the top to switch species
  • Drag with one finger to move around
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on your street
  • Tap a hexagon to learn more about that spot
