Biodiversity Map of the Month

This site contains maps telling different stories of bidodiversity in Melbourne, as part of the Map of the Month Collaboration between the Dr Kylie Sonas, Melbourne Data Analytics Platform, and Pursuit.

Available maps include:

1. Native Bees in the City

  • Native Bees
  • Blue-Banded Bee (Amegilla asserta)
  • Masked Bee (Hylaeus littleri)
  • Furrow Bee/Sweat Bee (Lasioglossum urbanum)
  • Metallic Green Nomia Bee (Lipotriches flavoviridis)
  • Leaf-Cutter Bee (Megachile apicata)
