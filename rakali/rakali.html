Rakali sighting recorded

Where the elusive rakali is hiding in Melbourne’s waterways

Each icon marks a sighting of a rakali, Australia’s native water rat, recorded across Greater Melbourne over the past 30 years.

How to explore

  • Drag with one finger to move around
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on your local waterway
  • Tap an icon to see details of that sighting
  • Look for the empty stretches. A gap may mean no rakali live there, or that nobody has spotted one yet.

Scan the QR code to read the full story on PursuitFrom Map of the Month, the University of Melbourne’s Pursuit series that turns research data into maps anyone can explore.
