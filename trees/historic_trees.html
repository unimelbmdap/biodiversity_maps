Reconstructing Melbourne’s 200-year-old forests

Each dot marks a tree that surveyors recorded on maps drawn between 1837 and 1855. The dots sit over today’s streets so you can see what grew here before the city did.

How to explore

  • Drag with one finger to move around
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in
  • Tap a dot to see the species, the survey date and the map it came from
